# Colombian Economics Journal
El objetivo de este portafolio es general aprendizaje hands-on usando proyectos de evaluacion de inversions, market perfomace y analsis de papers economicos 


## Project 1 — Macroeconomic & Stock Market Journal
- A continuous analysis of the COLCAP and S&P 500. Every two weeks, review performance and connect it to macroeconomic events, interest rates, inflation, commodities, currencies and risk sentiment. Every two months, investigate a major market trend; every four months, write a deeper paper on a long-term structural tendency.

- Purpose: learn to observe markets and connect price movements to the real economy.

## Project 2 — Economic Research Digest

- Read economic papers, break down their question, theory, methodology, data, findings and limitations, and explain them through a YouTube video and short GitHub recaps.

- Purpose: learn how professional economic research works and develop your ability to understand and communicate it.

## Project 3 — Business Valuation & Investment Lab

- Analyze real companies: understand the business, study its financial history, model its future performance, value it using methods such as DCF and multiples, and develop an investment thesis. 

- Purpose: develop fundamental investment and business-analysis skills.
